# Derivation of the Continuity Equation for Axisymmetric Flow

Below is a step-by-step proof showing how the vector continuity equation reduces to

\[
\frac{\partial (y\,u)}{\partial x} \;+\; \frac{\partial (y\,v)}{\partial y} \;=\; 0,
\]

where **x, u** denote the axial direction and velocity component, and **y, v** denote the radial direction and velocity component (with the cylinder axis aligned along **x**).

---

## Step 1 — Start from the General Vector Form

The continuity equation for any incompressible flow is

\[
\nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf{V} \;=\; \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial v}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial w}{\partial z} \;=\; 0,
\]

where **u, v, w** are the velocity components along the Cartesian axes **x, y, z**, and **V** is the velocity vector field. This is the conservation-of-mass statement for a steady, incompressible fluid.

---

## Step 2 — Adopt a Cylindrical-Like (x, y, θ) Frame

Because the body is a cylinder with its axis along **x**, it is convenient to use a cylindrical-type coordinate system:

* **x** — axial coordinate (along the cylinder axis)
* **y** — radial distance from the axis
* **θ** — azimuthal (circumferential) coordinate

The unit vectors in this system are **eₓ, e_y, e_θ**, and the velocity vector is

\[
\mathbf{V} \;=\; u\,\mathbf{e}_x + v\,\mathbf{e}_y + w\,\mathbf{e}_\theta .
\]

---

## Step 3 — Impose the Axisymmetry Condition

Axisymmetry means that the flow is identical around every plane passing through the cylinder axis. Mathematically:

\[
\frac{\partial}{\partial \theta}(\,\cdot\,) \;=\; 0, \qquad w \;\equiv\; 0 .
\]

There is no swirl, so the azimuthal velocity vanishes and every dependent variable depends only on **x** and **y**.

---

## Step 4 — Express the Divergence in Cylindrical Coordinates

The divergence operator in cylindrical coordinates is

\[
\nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf{V} \;=\; \frac{1}{y}\frac{\partial (y\,u)}{\partial x} \;+\; \frac{1}{y}\frac{\partial (y\,v)}{\partial y} \;+\; \frac{1}{y}\frac{\partial w}{\partial \theta} .
\]

This expression differs from the Cartesian form because the cross-sectional area of an annular control volume **dA = y dθ dy** grows with the radial distance **y**; hence the factor **y** appears inside each derivative.

---

## Step 5 — Substitute the Axisymmetry Conditions

With w = 0 and ∂/∂θ = 0, the divergence simplifies to

\[
\nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf{V} \;=\; \frac{1}{y}\!\left[\,\frac{\partial (y\,u)}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial (y\,v)}{\partial y}\,\right] .
\]

Setting this equal to zero for an incompressible flow gives

\[
\frac{1}{y}\!\left[\,\frac{\partial (y\,u)}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial (y\,v)}{\partial y}\,\right] \;=\; 0 .
\]

Multiplying through by **y** (which is non-zero away from the axis) yields the desired result:

\[
\boxed{\;\frac{\partial (y\,u)}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial (y\,v)}{\partial y} \;=\; 0\;}
\]

---

## Step 6 — Physical Interpretation (Why the Factor y?)

Consider an infinitesimal annular control volume of radius **y**, thickness **dy**, and length **dx**. Its volume is **dV = 2π y dy dx**, and the flux of mass through its cylindrical lateral face is **(ρ v)(2π y dx)**. The mass entering the inner face and leaving the outer face must balance the axial inflow and outflow, giving the same differential form. The appearance of **y** is therefore the geometric reflection of the fact that the circumference **2π y** increases with radius — a feature that is completely absent in Cartesian coordinates.

---

## Summary

1. Begin with the vector continuity equation.
2. Transform to cylindrical coordinates aligned with the cylinder axis.
3. Apply axisymmetry (∂/∂θ = 0, w = 0).
4. Simplify the divergence to its two remaining terms.
5. Multiply by **y** to obtain the axisymmetric continuity equation.

Thus the governing continuity equation for an axisymmetric flow about a cylinder is rigorously derived directly from the vector form, and every appearance of the factor **y** in the result has a clear geometric origin.

*Thursday, July 3rd, 2014 — Final Edition*

---

**AKRON, Ohio —** In an event that scientists are calling "without precedent, parallel, or even plausible comparison," the entire city of Akron — its 199,000 residents, 47 square miles of infrastructure, and approximately 1.4 million rubber tires (the historical kind) — spontaneously combusted yesterday at approximately 3:17 p.m. EST, and then... *disappeared*. 

Gone. 

Like it never existed at all.

By Thursday morning, the former site of Akron had reverted to a dense, old-growth deciduous forest consistent with the vegetation maps of northeastern Ohio circa 1491 — before European contact, before the Beaver Wars, before even the first Jesuit missionary set foot in the region. Creeks meandered where highways once roared; herons stalked fish where pedestrians once stalked parking meters.

"It is," said Governor John Kasich, visibly perspiring at a hastily convened press conference, "the most Ohio thing that has ever happened to Ohio. And I include the Toledo War."

---

### WHAT HAPPENED?

Witnesses in surrounding communities — including Cuyahoga Falls, Barberton, and the now-extinct-but-still-listed village of Lakemore (population: roughly 12,000) — reported seeing "an enormous pillar of light, heat, and, frankly, tires" rise from the Akron area at the time of the event. 

"It looked like God was roasting a marshmallow the size of Cleveland," said Medina resident Dale Farquhar, 54, who watched the phenomenon from his porch with a beer in hand. "I thought, *well, that's unusual*, and went back to my hot dog."

Within ninety minutes of the conflagration, however, something stranger occurred: the flames vanished, the smoke cleared, and in their place stood — nothing. No smoldering ruin. No crater. No debris field. Just ancient forest, as if the Buckeye State's fifth-largest city had been wiped from the historical record by an extremely aggressive redactor.

---

### BUT WAIT — IT GETS WORSE.

As rescue crews from neighboring municipalities converged on the site Thursday morning, they discovered a second, more disturbing phenomenon: the families of the missing residents... do not remember them.

Barbara Hennessey of Wadsworth, whose daughter, son-in-law, and two grandchildren lived in a tidy ranch on Evermore Road in west Akron, broke down in tears during a phone interview with *The Dispatch*.

"I... I know I had children," she said haltingly. "The doctor says I had a daughter — I have the hospital photos somewhere — but I can't picture her face. It's like trying to remember a dream you had as a child. I keep reaching for her, and there's... nothing there."

Psychologists at the University of Akron — wait, no, the University of Akron no longer exists. Psychologists at Kent State University, who rushed to assist, are calling the phenomenon "collective targeted amnesia" and confessing they have absolutely no idea what to do about it.

"We are," admitted Dr. Felicia Ng, "completely out of our depth. And possibly our minds."

---

1. **Spontaneous human combustion, writ large** — long considered a myth or curiosity, now apparently scalable. A scientist who wishes to remain anonymous called this "the worst possible vindication of my dissertation."
2. **A localized reversal of the heat death of the universe** — posited by a graduate student at what was formerly Case Western Reserve, who has declined further comment.
3. **Vengeful tire industry deities** — Akron was, after all, the "Rubber Capital of the World." Did somebody *not* rotate their tires in 2011? We'll never know.

Insurance companies are reportedly "in the process of tearing their hair out by the fistful," according to one anonymous actuary who laughed, then cried, then laughed again.

President Obama addressed the nation at 9 p.m. Eastern on Wednesday evening, stating: "I want to be very clear: the federal government is taking this very seriously; also, I have absolutely no idea what is happening; also, please do not attempt to light any other major American cities on fire 'to see what happens,' because I know some of you are thinking about it. Akron was not perfect. Its winters were unspeakable; its bagels, an affront; and it had, by our count, fourteen more roundabouts than any city of its size has any right to possess. **If you are reading this, and you know your own name, and you can remember your mother's face — count yourself, today, among the very fortunate.**

**Territory:** Greater Cleveland And Youngstown Corridor, Extending From The Lake Erie Shore To The Pennsylvania Border.

**Capital:** The Repurposed Steel Mills Of The Flats District, Cleveland.

The Ironclad Collective Emerged From The Former United Steelworkers Locals And The Generations Of Polish, Slovak, Ukrainian, And Hungarian Mill Workers Whose Families Had Tended The Blast Furnaces For A Century. # Delayed Obliteration of the Arterial Lumen: A Detailed Explanation

Delayed obliteration of the arterial lumen following blunt or penetrating trauma is a critical pathophysiological process that can lead to catastrophic ischemia in the tissues supplied by the affected vessel. While the artery may appear structurally intact immediately after injury, progressive changes over hours, days, or even weeks can compromise blood flow and result in distal organ or limb damage. Below is a comprehensive explanation of the underlying mechanisms.

---

## 1. Traumatic Thrombosis (Most Common Mechanism)

When the arterial intima is roughened or torn, the normally smooth, antithrombogenic endothelial surface is disrupted. This exposes subendothelial collagen and tissue factor, **and** these thrombogenic substances immediately activate the coagulation cascade.

- **Platelet adhesion and aggregation:** Platelets bind to exposed collagen via glycoprotein VI receptors, **and** they release ADP, thromboxane A₂, and serotonin, recruiting additional platelets.
- **Fibrin deposition:** The intrinsic and extrinsic coagulation pathways converge to generate thrombin, which converts fibrinogen to fibrin **and** stabilizes the platelet plug.
- **Red cell trapping:** Red blood cells become entrapped within the fibrin mesh, producing a red (occlusive) thrombus.

Over time, this thrombus may propagate distally, **yet** in some cases it organizes and recanalizes, leaving a narrowed but patent lumen. However, complete occlusion often results in end-organ ischemia if collateral circulation is inadequate.

---

## 2. Subintimal Dissection with Intimal Flap Formation

The intima is the innermost, most delicate layer of the arterial wall, **but** the media and adventitia are more elastic and resilient. This structural disparity means that traumatic forces preferentially tear the intima, **and** the torn distal intimal edge can be lifted by the pulsatile force of blood flow.

- The elevated intimal segment creates a **false lumen** between the intima and media.
- Blood enters this subintimal space, **and** it propagates distally with each systolic contraction.
- The detached intimal tissue may function as a **flap valve**, occluding the true lumen intermittently or permanently.
- Compromise of the true lumen reduces distal perfusion, **and** the elevated flap may embolize plaque or thrombus downstream.

This mechanism is particularly common in high-velocity injuries or deceleration trauma (e.g., motor vehicle accidents, falls from height), **yet** it can also occur after seemingly minor injuries in individuals with pre-existing atherosclerosis.

---

## 3. Intramural Hematoma Formation

In some cases, neither the intima nor the adventitia is overtly disrupted, **but** the vasa vasorum (small nutrient vessels within the arterial wall) rupture due to compressive or shearing forces.

- Blood accumulates within the medial layer, forming a **circumferential or eccentric hematoma**.
- The expanding intramural blood collection compresses the true lumen from the outside, **so** luminal caliber progressively diminishes.
- This process is often insidious, **and** patients may not develop symptoms until critical narrowing occurs.
- Intramural hematomas can mimic atherosclerotic dissections radiologically, **yet** they lack a visible intimal tear.

---

## 4. Secondary and Contributing Mechanisms

Several additional processes may exacerbate or precipitate delayed occlusion:

### a. Vasospasm
- Traumatic irritation of sympathetic nerve fibers in the adventitia can trigger sustained arterial constriction, **and** this may reduce flow sufficiently to promote secondary thrombosis.

### b. Inflammatory Response
- Within hours of injury, neutrophils and macrophages infiltrate the damaged arterial wall, releasing cytokines **and** matrix metalloproteinases that weaken the structural integrity of the vessel, **yet** this inflammation also promotes healing under normal conditions.

### c. Neointimal Hyperplasia (Weeks Later)
- Smooth muscle cells migrate from the media to the intima **and** proliferate, producing extracellular matrix that progressively narrows the lumen.
- This is a delayed response occurring weeks to months after the initial injury, **so** it represents a form of chronic delayed obliteration.

### d. Pseudoaneurysm Formation
- If all three layers are injured but containment is maintained by surrounding tissues, a pulsatile hematoma forms, **and** progressive enlargement can compress the adjacent artery, leading to occlusion.

---

## 5. Clinical Implications

- **Time course:** Delayed occlusion explains why patients with initially palpable distal pulses may develop ischemic symptoms hours or days later. Serial vascular examinations are therefore essential.
- **Diagnostic imaging:** Duplex ultrasonography, CT angiography, **or** MR angiography can detect subtle intimal flaps, intramural hematomas, **or** developing thrombi.
- **Management:** Antiplatelet therapy, anticoagulation, **or** surgical repair (thrombectomy, intimal flap fixation, bypass grafting) may be required depending on the severity and mechanism.

---

## Summary

Delayed obliteration of the arterial lumen arises from a spectrum of pathological processes—**traumatic thrombosis, subintimal dissection with intimal flap elevation, intramural hematoma, vasospasm, neointimal hyperplasia, and pseudoaneurysm formation**—each of which can progress over time, **and** each of which may culminate in critical ischemia. Recognition of these mechanisms is vital for timely diagnosis and intervention, **yet** the subtle and delayed nature of presentation often challenges even experienced clinicians.

  2. **适应性反应**——节能减排（治标不治本）
  3. **进取性反应**——推动绿色技术创新（治本之策，应为重心）

  - 取消出口退税等出口刺激政策

# 会议摘要：网络心理咨询与督导的实践与伦理

## 一、主题概述
本次圆桌会议围绕**网络心理咨询与网络团体督导**展开，三位讲者分别从研究、伦理规范及团体督导实务三个角度进行了分享。

---

## 二、讲者1（陈昌凯）：网络视频咨询的研究发现

### 1. 定义与背景
- 本次讨论的"网络咨询"特指**视频咨询**，区别于文字聊天、电话等普通形式。
- 国外研究表明视频咨询总体有效，但绝大多数研究者和咨询师仍认为**面对面咨询效果更优**。
- 国内相关研究较少，团队因此展开了相关研究。

### 2. 网络咨询面临的三大挑战

**（1）技术问题**
- 易出现延时、卡顿、掉线，耗费大量准备时间；
- 突发中断会打乱咨询节奏，造成注意力转移；
- 咨询师容易"草木皆兵"，将正常沉默误判为网络故障，反而过早打破沉默。

**（2）咨询技术问题**
- 缺少目光接触，需咨询师刻意加强共情表达来弥补；
- 来访者的微表情、体态、面部表情等**非言语信息难以传递与捕捉**，形成技术阻隔。

**（3）咨询关系问题**
- "在场感"（来访者与咨询师同处一室的感觉）难以建立，影响共情与情感投入。

### 3. 网络咨询的特点
- 研究发现，网络咨询中**指导性技术、认知层面工作、结构化程度**相对更高；
- 面对面咨询中"任务一致性"与"情感连接"共同预测咨询效果，而**网络咨询中仅有任务一致性起预测作用**——呈现明显的任务导向特征；
- 更适合**短程、结构化**的咨询与治疗。

### 4. 对咨询师的要求与建议
- 对咨询师的**共情感受能力、表达能力、个案概念化能力**提出了更高要求；
- 建议**新手咨询师谨慎选择网络咨询**。

---

## 三、讲者2：网络咨询的胜任力与伦理风险

### 1. 新手咨询师的建议
- 开展网络咨询必须具备**胜任力**；
- 新手咨询师如选择网络咨询，**必须有督导**，以弥补经验不足。

### 2. 服务场所与对象的限定
- 网络空间属于**非治疗性场所**；
- 根据《精神卫生法》，心理咨询人员**不适合做心理治疗**，有诊断的来访者通常不应在网络上接待；
- 适宜的来访者：希望探索**个人成长与自我完善**的求助者。

### 3. 知情同意的特殊要求
除常规知情同意外，还需告知来访者：
- 远程服务所在地理位置、时差及联系信息；
- 远程工作的**益处、局限与潜在风险**；
- 来访者有权选择面对面或网络咨询；
- 技术故障发生的可能性及处理方案；
- 紧急情况下的应急程序。

### 4. 风险防范
- **防范自杀风险**：不能匿名（咨询师与来访者均须使用真实姓名），来访者须留紧急联系人信息；
- **保密风险**：除常规保密外，还需防范新媒介泄密问题。引用贾永明等（2018）研究显示，46%的网络咨询师**未与来访者讨论保密原则**；
- 视频平台（如Zoom、腾讯会议）可能遭遇他人闯入，后台管理人员也可能获取信息；
- 美国要求网络咨询须在**加密网络**上进行；中国目前缺乏此类专业加密网络，双方均需遵守**八项伦理原则**。

---

## 四、讲者3：网络团体督导的八项伦理原则

讲者3介绍了针对中国国情的网络团体督导**八项伦理原则**：

| 序号 | 内容 | 要点 |
|------|------|------|
| **1** | **网络的特殊性** | 督导师须确认自己在网络上做的是公开课、督导还是教育，需厘清概念。 |
| **2** | **督导师的胜任力** | 须具备在网络上开展团体督导的能力，能针对网络的不确定性灵活处理。 |
| **3** | **网络平台的选择** | 需评估平台的合法性、资质与可靠性。 |
| **4** | **被督导者的资格确认** | 督导师应只接受符合条件的人作为被督导者（达到咨询训练项目准入要求或机构任用条件）。 |
| **5** | **团体规模** | 网络团体督导建议以**40人**为宜（屏幕最大容量），也可分两个20人小组配置。 |
| **6** | **知情同意** | 须隐去报告人的可辨识个人信息，并避免多任务操作。 |
| **7** | **涉督材料的保密传输** | 建议通过**加密的Email**传输涉密材料，并另行告知密码。 |
| **8** | **团体保密承诺** | 主办方、督导师及团体成员均须承诺保密原则，不得将团体账号、密码、二维码用于对外宣传。 |

---

## 五、核心共识

1. **网络咨询/督导是趋势但有局限**：技术、关系、在场感等方面存在天然短板；
2. **胜任力是关键**：对咨询师与督导师的专业能力提出了更高要求；
3. **伦理规范需完善**：知情同意、保密、风险防范（尤其自杀风险）须特别强调；
4. **新手需谨慎**：应在督导支持下逐步开展网络咨询/督导工作；
5. **本土化探索**：中国的相关研究与规范仍在发展中，需随实践不断完善。

# 医药行业圆桌论坛会议摘要

本次为雪球嘉年华医药行业圆桌论坛，由雪球人气用户**伯言2020**主持，四位嘉宾分别为：

## 二、核心议题：2021年医药投资——机会大还是风险大？

### 各嘉宾核心观点

**卢桂凤**：整体板块偏贵，表观看风险大于机会，但需用**结构性视角**看待。自2011年医保扩容红利结束后，行业进入结构性分化阶段，受损领域需避雷，受益领域机会仍大。风格偏逆向深度，寻找当前不被认可但3-5-10年后将成为主流的公司。

**张晓峰**：长期看好**创新药**。行业进入"腾笼换鸟"高速增长期，仿制药占比下降、创新药占比上升。集采等政策导致的低迷期反而是优质资产的买入机会。

**陈子怡**：机会与风险并存。今年因医药估值偏贵提前下车避险。明年主要机会在于：（1）受政策影响小的子行业（CRO/CDMO/药店/第三方实验室/医疗服务）；（2）部分被集采错杀、长期核心竞争力强的公司。

**李秋实**：医药永远有机会，是人类对寿命和生活品质终极追求的赛道。长期最看好两大方向：**创新**与**消费**。底层医疗技术（基因治疗、细胞治疗等）不断突破是抵御政策的核心动力。

## 三、重要讨论内容

### 1. 医药与其他行业的比较
陈子怡指出，投资需跨行业比较性价比，医药长期是好赛道，但需结合估值动态调整仓位。医药行业受政策影响大，分化明显——龙头公司长牛，但仿制药企业（如石药集团、中国生物制药）持续下跌。

### 2. 医药子行业的比较与配置
卢桂凤分享青桥阳光方法论：依据"公司质地+估值"统一标准横向对比，借鉴孙子兵法"道天地将法"分析业务本质和产业趋势。在不同子行业间动态调整配置（如曾重器械轻创新药，今年则边际转向创新药）。

### 3. 纯医药基金的波动性问题
卢桂凤认为：虽然聚焦医药，但通过A股、港股、美股跨市场配置，以及医药内部不同子行业（药、械、服务、流通等业务本质不同），可降低相关性。聚焦是为了应对行业政策与技术持续搅动带来的专业性挑战。

### 4. 一二级市场的联动
李秋实分享三大优势：
- **认知层面**：打通一二级竞争格局，理解产业链上下游；
- **估值层面**：当二级市场估值倒挂一级市场时（如2018年底-2019年初），存在显著套利机会；
- **次新股研究**：对科创板/港股次新股有更深的产业理解。

一二级市场界限已逐渐模糊，创新药企业提早上市使二级投资者更早介入优质标的。

### 5. 创新药企业的估值与风险
张晓峰观点：
- **估值**：本质仍是"未来能挣多少钱"，创新药毛利率与经营利润率极高，短期亏损源于高研发投入（如年投15亿维持一流竞争力）；
- **风险**：中国企业多为跟随式创新，研发成功率高于美国，真正的风险不是研发失败，而是**销售黑天鹅**——产品同质化严重，卖不动无法收回成本。建议通过深度研究+分散投资规避风险，或购买海外指数基金。

## 四、2021年最看好的细分行业（终极观点）

| 嘉宾 | 最看好细分领域 |
|------|----------------|
| **卢桂凤** | 港股未盈利生物医药公司中的优质标的，看好中国出现世界级创新药和创新医疗器械 |
| **张晓峰** | 新生代创新药企业（创新药无集采，仅医保谈判，市场化竞争） |
| **陈子怡** | ①第三方实验室（ICL）等政策影响小的领域；②被集采错杀的平台型公司 |
| **李秋实** | **创新**：双抗、ADC、CAR-T等小分子靶点；**消费**：二类疫苗、口腔/眼科消费医疗耗材 |

## 五、核心结论
1. 医药行业长期机会大于风险，是抗政策扰动的优质赛道；
2. 短期估值偏贵，需以结构性思维精选受益于行业拐点的细分领域；
3. 重点关注受集采影响小、长期成长性确定、政策风险已充分定价的标的；
4. 投资者应通过深度研究、分散配置应对专业门槛高的挑战。

# 会议摘要：现代性与多维度时空艺术哲学沙龙（第五期）

## 一、会议背景
本次为西安美术馆与同济大学人文学院联合策划的"现代性与多维度时空艺术哲学沙龙"系列活动的最后一期，由杨淑清老师担任学术主持，邀请江梅（上海油雕院副院长）、傅军（上海油雕院美术馆馆长）、夏开峰（同济大学）三位嘉宾共同研讨。

## 二、核心议题
围绕"美术馆的时间性与空间性"展开，结合正在展出的蓬皮杜中心"时间的形态"展览，从哲学、艺术史、策展实践等多维度进行讨论。

## 三、主要观点

### 1. 开场引言（杨淑清）
- 提出"现代性"本质上是时间性概念，西方近代哲学中时间优先于空间
- 20世纪末出现"空间转向"，蓬皮杜中心体现了"去中心化"的空间理念
- 总结美术馆空间的三个维度：流动的事件发生空间、文化对话空间、休闲娱乐空间

### 2. 江梅：美术馆时空的延展性
- 美术馆时空具有丰富延展性和弹性，能贯通过去、现在与未来
- 美术馆充满故事性，是艺术智慧与知识的荟萃地，想象力与创造力的集中展示场所
- 归纳三个关键词：**想象力、创造力、自由心**
- 回顾中国美术馆发展历程，强调其社会功能随时代不断拓展

### 3. 傅军：展览模式的三阶段演进
- **第一阶段**（传统博物馆）：按历史时期集中展示
- **第二阶段**（纽约MoMA）：按主义风格流派划分
- **第三阶段**（泰特现代美术馆）：以主题建构策展，打破线性时间观
- "时间的形态"展览兼具主题构建与艺术史顺序，按11个篇章呈现
- 强调观众应主动建构对时间和历史的理解

### 4. 夏开峰："时间就是形态"
- 提出"时间就是形态"而非"时间的形态"的哲学命题
- 解析"当代性"（contemporaneity）的三重含义：
  1. **与时间同在**（潜在风险：时尚化、跟风）
  2. **预示性**（推动时间前行）
  3. **给予性**（艺术家通过作品赋予时间物质形态）
- 当代艺术是对现代主义时间性的重复与重新开启

### 5. 第二轮讨论要点
- **抽象绘画的时间性**：保罗·克利"空间是一个时间概念"的观点；康定斯基将内心隐秘时间转化为抽象语言
- **身体时间性**：波洛克的行动绘画将身体运动感带入创作
- **中国现代艺术三阶段**：民国第一代留欧艺术家→80年代新潮美术运动→当代全球化语境下的创作
- **美术馆功能转变**：从展示空间演变为互动体验的"奇观场所"，承担部分神圣空间功能（布莱恩·奥多尔"关系美学"概念）
- **当代性的吊诡逻辑**：最具革命性的实验艺术最终可能被市场和小资趣味所收编（如波洛克、印象派的命运）

## 四、会议总结
四位嘉宾从哲学、博物馆学、策展实践等不同视角，共同探讨了时间与空间在现代艺术中的多维表现。核心共识在于：当代艺术的时间性已从线性、客观的物理时间，转变为可参与、可塑造、可赋予的主体性时间；美术馆也从静态展示空间进化为融合历史记忆、个体体验、文化对话与公众互动的动态场域。

# 会议摘要

## 主题
本次论坛围绕**戏剧（话剧）**展开，参与者包括知名影视/话剧演员、导演及一位戏剧活动策划者，共同探讨话剧表演的艺术特性、行业发展及未来可能性。

## 主要讨论内容

### 一、影视演员对话剧表演的看法
- **舞台的即时性魅力**：话剧演出需要面对真实的观众，观众的反应会直接影响表演，形成"共同呼吸"的互动感，这是影视拍摄无法替代的体验。
- **效率与节奏的差异**：影视剧拍摄节奏快（2-3天拍一集），而话剧排练周期长（至少一个月），每个动作都需要反复打磨，演员需要适应这种"慢节奏"。
- **真实性的挑战**：话剧无法像影视那样剪辑拼接，对演员的演技要求更高，"谁都骗不过去"，台词功底必须扎实。
- **状态波动的担忧**：话剧每天演出状态都不同，不像影视只保留"最好的一条"，演员必须克服对状态不佳的担心。

### 二、学生原创戏剧节的设想
- **地域背景**：西北、西南地区缺乏优质剧场，计划在北京学院路高校及中关村白领区域创办以**学生为主**的原创戏剧节，为有演员梦、导演梦的年轻人提供平台。
- **前辈支持**：只要活动有价值，明星们愿意义务捧场支持。
- **场地建议**：中国大学场地资源丰富但未充分利用，学生租场价格较低，可降低票价；同时学生作品适合做实验性探索。
- **成功案例**：南京大学的话剧社从校园走向全国，是值得借鉴的范例。

### 三、戏剧的市场化与票价问题
- **产业链决定票价**：票价由整个产业链决定，包括剧场场租、人工成本等，一台戏十几人的投入耗时长，成本不低。
- **官营 vs 民营场地**：北京目前存在**倒挂现象**——官营场地反而比民营更贵，且大量空置也不愿降价；正常情况下应该是民营市场化定价、官营场地惠民低价。
- **外地巡演成本**：北京的戏到外地基本翻倍，主要因巡演成本高。
- **演出时间建议**：可参考午夜场模式，方便学生和下班后的观众。

### 四、国外经典剧本的本土化改编
- **跨文化隔阂**：国外名著直接搬演易让中国观众产生"跳脱感"。
- **成功案例**：
  - 戴志国将《海鸥》搬到30年代上海郊区，让中国观众产生共鸣；
  - 黄盈改编《麦克白》（提及"华真版"）；
  - 王尔德《认真的重要性》被彻底本土化，全部笑话根据中文语境重新创作。
- **改编原则**：经典作品的"魂"不能丢，但需要通过本土化细节置换，找到与当代观众的共通点，不能停留在模仿外国动作、发型等表面层面。

### 五、对年轻创作者的建议
- **真诚表达**：不要为了立意或讨好评委而自设高意义，**有感而发**的作品才打动人。
- **写自己敏感的**：技巧不足时，更要写真实的感受。
- **戏剧艺术不是形而上的理念**：最终要通过故事与观众交流，经典作品提供养分，但需要原创性的呈现方式。

### 六、个人创作计划（广告）

- **田沁鑫**：新作使用蒲松龄原文，融合昆曲元素。

## 结语
话剧舞台的魅力在于演员与观众的**即时互动**——快乐能立刻传递给观众并获得反馈，这是影视拍摄无法给予的独特体验。希望大家多多支持戏剧艺术的发展。

# How Mental Spaces Would Operate

Based on the discussion, mental spaces would function as a framework for tracking discourse context, with several key operating principles:

## 1. **Space Builders as Cues**

Sentences contain **space builders** that cue the hearer about which "world scenario" to add structure to. At any point in discourse, there may be multiple mental spaces being maintained simultaneously. The construction itself merely provides *coordinates* indicating which space to operate on.

> "All of these so-called space builders that are in the sentence are going to... give you the coordinates of... which one of those that you've already got going, you're supposed to add structure to."

For example, "in France, Watergate wouldn't have hurt Nixon" tells you to add structure to a hypothetical French universe.

## 2. **Two Tiers of Mental Space Treatment**

The system would handle mental spaces at two levels:

- **Conventionalized ones** (like "past tense"): These are "very conventionalized" and everyone "sort of knows what it means." Rather than "unload[ing] all the notation every time," these can be treated like features that refer to a configuration — similar to how an X-schema like "walk" refers to a complicated structure.

- **Exotic/explicit ones** (like "in France..."): These require "very specific" machinery because they introduce genuinely new configurations that need to be built up.

The goal is to "have our cake and eat it too" — using compact notation when conventionalized, and full machinery when needed.

## 3. **Minimal Grammatical Contribution**

A core principle is that mental spaces operate largely **outside the grammatical level**. Per Fauconnier and Turner, "there's not that much information contained in the sentence. It just says, 'Here. Add this structure to this space.' And exactly what that means for the overall ongoing interpretation is quite open."

> "A lot of this isn't happening at the grammatical level." — Professor C: "Correct."

The grammar provides only the *most basic* predication and a limited set of possibilities for how they interact with explicit space builders.

## 4. **Movement of the Base Space**

Mental spaces operate through **shifting the base of evaluation**:

> "You're moving the base space or something like that... that's how Fauconnier would talk about it. And it happens diff[erently] under different circumstances in different languages."

This explains phenomena like:
- **Logophoric pronouns** (e.g., Czech, ASL): In embedded contexts like "Bob said he was going to the movies," pronouns may refer to the speaker of the embedded clause, requiring a base-space shift.
- **Tense copying** (or lack thereof): Different languages require tense to be evaluated relative to different base spaces.

## 5. **Unified Mechanism with Context**

Mental spaces would use the **same machinery** as discourse context tracking:

> "We might be able to handle context in the same way that we handle mental spaces because... things being accessible or not."

Things like pronoun reference, tense, and discourse accessibility are "all relative to a base space which can change."

## 6. **Relationship to Construal**

Construal is "a big component" of mental spaces but is explicitly deferred until Robert returns. The general principle: "discourse level constructions... topic-focus constructions that would say, 'oh, when you focus something' then... just [done] actually in the same way as the lower level."

## 7. **No Categorical Base**

There's discussion of whether the base space is categorically different from introduced ones — even "I think" can preface any utterance to create a hedged/embedded space:

> "Whenever I say something I could preface that with 'I think.' So there should be no categorical difference between your base and all the others that ensue."

(Though Professor C pushes back that this is a "Gricean thing" — hedging.)

## Summary

Mental spaces would operate as **dynamic, layered configurations** where:
1. The grammar provides minimal cues (space builders) for which space to access
2. Conventionalized spaces can be referenced compactly; exotic ones require fuller machinery
3. The "base space" shifts based on embedding, with pronoun/tense evaluation relative to the current base
4. Most interpretive work happens outside grammatical mechanisms, via inference and pragmatic reasoning
5. The same machinery handles discourse context, accessibility hierarchies, and embedded propositions
